BFSI Powerhouse

HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Axis Bank, Kotak, Reliance Capital — Mumbai's BFSI sector hires more MIS, reporting, and data analytics professionals than any other industry cluster in India.

Amazon India, Myntra, Meesho, Nykaa — many have Mumbai-based analytics hubs focused on customer behaviour, demand forecasting, and supply chain data — all ideal for our graduates.
